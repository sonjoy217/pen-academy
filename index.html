What is Penetration Testing?

Penetration Testing (or Pen Testing) is a legal and authorized cyberattack performed on a computer system to find security vulnerabilities. It follows a systematic process:

  • Reconnaissance: Gathering data about the target.
  • Scanning: Identifying open ports and services.
  • Exploitation: Gaining access to the system.
  • Reporting: Documenting findings to fix security gaps.
